For in-game icon design, we should prioritize:

- **Strong, recognizable silhouettes** that read clearly at small sizes.
    
- **High visual contrast** and clear lighting/specular highlights.
    
- **Simplified, bold subjects** that fill the tile area without busy background elements or micro-details.
    
- **Color language** that immediately communicates the material (e.g., distinct metallic tones, copper oranges, iron grays, high-visibility hazard accents).

Aligning with **Krastorio 2 (K2)** and vanilla **Factorio** gives us a very clear, established visual benchmark to target.

Krastorio 2 has a crisp, punchy aesthetic that blends seamlessly with vanilla Factorio while using vibrant color-coding and highly distinct material identities.

### Key Design Changes for Native In-Game Icons

To ensure your new items look like official Factorio / Krastorio 2 assets in inventory slots, assembling machines, logistics networks, and belts, we need to adapt our approach:

1. **Remove the Foundation Plate Entirely**
    
    - Factorio item icons float directly over inventory grid squares. A baked-in metal pedestal or circuit plate bakes in unnecessary noise and shrinks the actual item inside the 64x64px tile.
        
    - Eliminating the base plate allows the subject itself to fill 80–90% of the tile frame.
        
2. **Strong Silhouettes & Dark Outer Edges**
    
    - Items need crisp, dark outer outlines or heavy ambient shading on the perimeter so they remain easily legible against dark gray inventory UI slots, belts, and bright map overlays.
        
3. **Krastorio 2 Material Color Language**
    
    - **Crushed Iron Ore**: Cool metallic blue-gray gravel pile with sharp specular highlights.
        
    - **Crushed Copper Ore**: Rich reddish-orange metallic ore chunks.
        
    - **Crushed Rare Metals**: Deep metallic purple/violet crystalline ore (matching K2’s signature Rare Metals palette).
        
    - **Crushed Uranium Ore**: Dark charcoal stone fragments with vivid, glowing lime-green radioactive veins/flecks.
        
    - **Wood-Pulp (Fluid)**: Pale organic tan/cream liquid droplet or fluid container icon with fibrous texture details.
        
    - **Liquid Waste (Fluid)**: Murky greenish-brown polluted sludge teardrop/fluid droplet with chemical sheen.
        
    - **Bakelite**: Dark reddish-brown / amber-marbled vintage synthetic resin (smooth, glossy cylinder, sheet, or ingot).
        
    - **Nitrocellulose**: Fluffy off-white / pale yellow fibrous cake or hazardous material pack.

4. **Fluid vs. Solid Icon Standards**
    
    - **Fluids** in Factorio use the standard stylized droplet/teardrop icon or fluid barrel icon so players instantly distinguish liquids from solid items on belts and assembly recipes.
        
    - **Solids / Ores** use 3/4 isometric perspective piles, ingots, or blocks.
