## Core Base Game Categories

- **`"crafting"`** – Standard items made in the hand or standard assembling machines.
- **`"hand-crafting"`** – Recipes explicitly restricted to or allowed in the player's personal inventory (replaces old manual behaviors).
- **`"advanced-crafting"`** – Advanced assembly recipes (e.g., Engine units).
- **`"crafting-with-fluid"`** – Recipes requiring a fluid input/output in an assembly machine.
- **`"smelting"`** – Items processed inside standard Furnaces.
- **`"chemistry"`** – Recipes handled specifically by the Chemical Plant.
- **`"oil-processing"`** – Crude oil refining steps assigned to the Oil Refinery.
- **`"centrifuging"`** – Uranium processing and nuclear cycling inside the Centrifuge.
- **`"rocket-building"`** – Rocket parts constructed inside the Rocket Silo. 

## Space Age & Specialized Planet Categories

- **`"metallurgy"`** – Foundry-specific casting and advanced metal processing (Vulcanus).
- **`"electromagnetics"`** – High-tech processing handled by the Electromagnetic Plant (Fulgora).
- **`"cryogenics"`** – Low-temperature chemical handling inside the Cryogenic Plant (Aquilo).
- **`"organic"`** – Bio-organic processing assigned to the Biochamber (Gleba).
- **`"crushing"`** – Solid crushing behaviors (e.g., Scrap crushing).
- **`"recycling"`** – Dissolving items back into resources via the Recycler (moved to its own quality/recycling sub-module).
- **`"captive-spawner-process"`** – Unique biological processes interacting with enemy spawners.
