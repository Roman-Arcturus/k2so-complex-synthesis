The intent of the mod is to add more depth to Krastorio2. There will be minimal new ingredients made from the same Factorio/K2 primal resources. There are plenty of mods that introduce new ores, metals and minerals, but the downside of them is that many recipes create one-use ingredient or something that becomes obsolete as the game progresses. 

With Space Age, I also want to make [[planet_Nauvis]] a planet that has it's unique production chain that is required to be maintained until endgame.

[[planet_Nauvis]] unique resources:
* [[uranium-ore]] - Nuclear power
	→ [[uranium-238]]
	→ [[uranium-235]]
- [[kr-rare-metals]] - An umbrella for all kind of rare and precious metals
- [[kr-imersite]] - Endgame resource
	→ [[kr-imersite-powder]]
- [[kr-mineral-water]]
	→ [[fluid_kr-nitric-acid]]
	→  [[kr-lithium-chloride]]
		→  [[kr-lithium]]

[[planet_Nauvis]] has [[rc_rx_kr-greenhouse]] tech, so we need to make it an important and irreplaceable resource until [[planet_Gleba]] is secured.
