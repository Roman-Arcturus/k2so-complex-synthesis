#### 📜 Thematic description:
Hydrating and pressure-filtering crushed wood chips separates organic cellulose fibres into a dense liquid pulp matrix. This mechanical extraction strips away organic impurities, yielding liquid wood pulp for chemical synthesis alongside concentrated biomass.
