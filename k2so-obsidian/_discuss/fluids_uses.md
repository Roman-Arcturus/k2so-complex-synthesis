Restricting RX-specific additions on Nauvis to just two umbrella fluids (**`fluid_rx-wood-pulp`** and **`fluid_rx-liquid-waste`**) strictly enforces the "depth over width" design goal. Instead of diluting logistics with endless single-purpose liquids, this tight roster forces existing K2/vanilla fluids into heavy, competing roles across all three production tiers.

### The Functional Matrix of Nauvis Fluids

```
[Raw Resources / Chemistry]
│
├─► Forestry Branch 
│	─► wood ─► rx-wood-pulp ─► rx-bakelite / rx-guncotton
│
├─► Aqueous Chemistry 
│	─► kr-mineral-water ─► Glass Flux / Pulp Digestion / Leaching
│
├─► Atmospheric Gas Loop 
│	─► kr-nitrogen / oxygen ─► Acid Nitration / T3 Smelting
│
├─► Tier 3 Processing 
│	─► [6 Input + Fluids] ─► 12 Output + rx-liquid-waste
│
└─► Tier 3 Waste
	─► rx-liquid-waste ─► kr-dirty-water ──► Stone/Sand
```

### 1. Organic & Forestry Chain (`fluid_rx-wood-pulp`)

- **Role:** Acts as the primary liquid gateway for all wood-derived chemistry.
- **Interconnections:**
    - Digested from wood chips using `fluid_water` (basic yield) or `fluid_kr-mineral-water` (enhanced yield + biomass output).
        
    - Refined into `item_kr-biomass` to bootstrap early military science and supply `facility_kr-bio-lab`.
        
    - Combined with `fluid_kr-nitric-acid` / `fluid_sulfuric-acid` to produce guncotton ($\text{nitrocellulose}$) for advanced ammunition and military payloads.

### 2. Tier 3 Effluent Cascade (`fluid_rx-liquid-waste`)

- **Role:** Operates as the single umbrella liquid waste generated by high-yield Tier 3 smelting and complex chemical extraction.
    
- **Interconnections:**
    - Contains residual acids, unreacted chemical solvents, and trace minerals from Tier 3 recipes.
    - Processed in Stage 1 waste reclamation blocks to recover concentrated reagents (like `fluid_sulfuric-acid`, `fluid_kr-hydrogen-chloride`, or trace rare-metal slurries).
    - Degrades into standard **`fluid_kr-dirty-water`** as its spent tailing, which then flows into Stage 2 filtration to precipitate sand and stone.

### 3. Aqueous Chemistry (`fluid_kr-mineral-water`)

- **Role:** Serves as Nauvis's irreplaceable liquid reagent, ensuring Nauvis remains an essential industrial hub even in late-game _Space Age_.
    
- **Interconnections:**
    - Used as a high-efficiency digestion liquor to accelerate `fluid_rx-wood-pulp` production.
    - Acts as a liquid flux component for Tier 2/3 glass and silicon refining.
    - Applied in quartz leaching and slag washing to recover trace minerals before ore enrichment.

### 4. Gas & Nitration Infrastructure

The existing K2 gas roster handles the chemical weight for high-tier metallurgy and military logistics without needing extra RX gases:

- **`fluid_kr-nitrogen` & `fluid_kr-nitric-acid`:** Extracted via atmospheric condensers (fed by `item_k2-air-filter`) to feed guncotton nitration and high-tier chemical steps.
    
- **`fluid_kr-hydrogen-chloride`:** Direct fluid reagent for Tier 3 high-purity glass and silicon industrial furnaces.
    
- **`fluid_kr-ammonia` & `fluid_kr-biomethanol`:** Feed organic synthesis, synthetic resin routes, and secondary fuel loops.
    

### Logistical Impact

Because the fluid list is compact, Transport Drone depots and train terminals on Nauvis only need to manage a few core liquid networks. High recipe complexity comes entirely from how these exact fluids are routed, combined, and recovered between city-blocks.
