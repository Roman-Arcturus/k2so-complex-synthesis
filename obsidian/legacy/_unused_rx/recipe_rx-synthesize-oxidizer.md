#### 📜 Thematic description:
Combining concentrated oxygen, nitric acid, and fine carbon dust in an electrolysis chamber creates a highly reactive solid oxidizer. This dense compound is essential for driving ultra-high-temperature smelting and advanced metallurgical reduction processes.
