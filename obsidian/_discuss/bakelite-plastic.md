# 🧪 Material Strategy: Synthetic Polymers vs. Thermoset Matrices

To establish a clear industrial and technological boundary between `[[plastic-bar]]` and `[[rx-bakelite]]`, we must define their physical roles based on early-game scalability versus mid-to-late-game high-performance demands. 

In standard Krastorio 2, `[[plastic-bar]]` is a catch-all hydrocarbon used for everything from low-tech pipes to high-tech computing matrices. By splitting these roles, you force a beautiful industrial shift: early-game plastic remains cheap and highly scalable for simple structural goods, while your custom `[[rx-bakelite]]` acts as the premium, heat-resistant, high-insulation material required to survive your **3x technology cost** progression.

---

## 📐 The Material Matrix Boundaries

### 1. 🟥 [[item_plastic-bar]] (Early Synthetic Hydrocarbon)
* **Pacing & Availability**: Unlocked immediately upon refining `[[petroleum-gas]]`. Highly automated, linear, single-stage mass production.
* **Thematic Characteristics**: Flexible, lightweight, easily molded, but completely unstable under high electrical voltage or extreme thermodynamic stress.
* **Refactored Roles**: Restricted strictly to high-volume mechanical commodities, fluids, and non-conductive mechanical items:
	- All basic logistic belts, inserter casings, and transport drone chassis.
	- Fluid handling: Chemical pipes, basic storage tanks, and battery casings.
	- Low-tier cargo containers and standard explosive casings.

### 2. 🟫 [[item_rx-bakelite]] (Advanced Thermoset Insulation Grid)
* **Pacing & Availability**: Requires an intricate cross-mod assembly chain combining Nauvis automated forestry arrays, filtration loops, and specialized chemical acid synthesis.
* **Thematic Characteristics**: Rigid, brittle, completely flame-retardant, non-deformable, and a absolute non-conductor under multi-kilovolt stress.
* **Refactored Roles**: Becomes the mandatory, foundational structural layer for all high-voltage electrical, computing, and vacuum planetary systems:
	- **Electronics**: Overhauling the baseline substrate grids for `[[electronic-circuit]]` (Green) and `[[advanced-circuit]]` (Red).
	- **Power Distribution**: Required to mold heavy insulation housings for accumulators, substations, big electric poles, and transformers.
	- **Structural Shielding**: Used as a rigid stabilizer binding compound inside your advanced `[[rx-enriched-steel-mix]]` and advanced chemical refineries.

---

## 🗺️ The Progression Shift

By enforcing these boundaries, you create a dynamic progression path:
1. **The Plastic Era (Yellow/Red Science)**: The base runs on oil. Plastics are routed to build out thousands of belts, bots, and standard logistic city-blocks.
2. **The Bakelite Era (Blue/Yellow Science & Pre-Space)**: As high-voltage power networks and computing demands skyrocket under the 3x tech penalty, the factory experiences a major shift. The player cannot simply scale up raw plastic to progress; they must establish specialized biological-chemical blocks to mass-produce Bakelite, cleanly transitioning your factory rows into a premium electronics era.
