#### 📜 Thematic description:
Raw timber is too structurally dense for chemical synthesis. Mechanical crushing shatters rigid fibres into high-surface-area wood chips, making organic materials easily soluble for pulp extraction, seed germination, and bio-polymer manufacturing.

#### 💎Result:
