#### 📜 Thematic description:
Bulk coal lacks the uniform reactivity needed for high-tier synthesis. Pulverizing raw coal into fine carbon dust maximizes surface area and thermal conductivity, transforming crude mineral fuel into an essential reduction agent for advanced metallurgy, chemistry, and electrolysis.

#### 💎Result:
Process result is [[item_rx-crushed-carbon]]. It should replace raw coal from most but the basic recipes.
